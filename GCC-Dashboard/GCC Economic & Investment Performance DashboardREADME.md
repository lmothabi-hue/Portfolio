Project title: 

Gaborone City Council — Economic & Investment Performance Dashboard

Description: 

A comprehensive Power BI dashboard built using data from the World Bank, Statistics Botswana, and GCC Budget Reports (2018–2024). 

The dashboard tracks:

Revenue collected vs target (BWP 1,499M collected against a BWP 1,620M target — 92.53% collection rate)

Quarterly revenue performance from Q1 2022 to Q4 2024, with a goal of 150 exceeded by +7.14%

Budget vs infrastructure funding gap analysis across fiscal years 2018–2025

An infrastructure projects tracker covering 10 projects totalling BWP 1,016M estimated cost, with a BWP 487M funding gap

GCC revenue streams by source (property rates, ground leases, advertising, parking, service levies, govt grants) from 2018–2024

Key insights surfaced by the dashboard include;

P487M total funding gap across 10 projects

Infrastructure need outpacing budget every year

Government grants accounting for 57% of total GCC revenue.

⚠ Note: Budget and infrastructure figures are estimates based on publicly available GCC reports. Data is intended for portfolio demonstration purposes.
